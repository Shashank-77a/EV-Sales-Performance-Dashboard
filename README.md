# EV-Sales-Performance-Dashboard
An interactive EV Sales Performance Dashboard built to practice data visualization and analysis, utilizing an AI-generated dataset to track revenue, vehicle classes, and brand metrics.

This repository contains an Electric Vehicle (EV) sales performance dashboard created as a personal practice project to develop and refine my data visualization and analytics skills in Power BI. 

**Note:** The dataset powering this dashboard (`EV Sales Data.xlsx`) was entirely AI generated to simulate realistic market conditions for practice purposes.

##  Key Features & Insights
The dashboard tracks major EV market KPIs and segments the data for deeper analysis:
* **High-Level Financials:** Tracks $9.4bn in Gross Revenue and $7.2bn in Gross Profit, alongside YoY and MoM growth metrics.
* **Sales Volume:** Monitors 186K Total Units Sold and 50K Total Orders with an overall profit margin of 76.49%.
* **Trend Analysis:** Features a time-series revenue trend line evaluating performance across yearly, quarterly, and monthly intervals from 2019 to 2023.
* **Brand & Model Breakdown:** Analyzes units sold by vehicle class (Hatchback, Sedan, SUV, Truck) and individual brand revenue share, comparing top performers like Tesla, Ford, Chevrolet, Kia, and Rivian.
* **Technical Specs Correlation:** Maps the relationship between charging types, battery capacity (kWh), and range (miles).
* **Geospatial Distribution:** Visualizes gross revenue across various US states.
